## Policy on sources

- Facts stated as established in the film are backed by S1 and S2 jointly, or by the
  1970 police record as relayed by them.
- Single-source or anonymous claims (taxi driver 1991, Fløyen hiker 2005, Forbach
  informant 2019, NZZ 2023) are labelled as such on screen and in CLAIMS.md.
- No dialogue is invented; every quotation is paraphrase-free or clearly marked as a
  paraphrase. No forensic conclusions beyond the autopsy record are asserted.
