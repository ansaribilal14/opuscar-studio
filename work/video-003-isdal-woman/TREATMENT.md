# TREATMENT.md — video-003 The Isdal Woman

## Logline

In November 1970, a burned body was found in Norway's "Death Valley". Her labels were
cut off, her names were false, and fifty-five years later the world still cannot say
who she was. This is the evidence-first account of the case that Norwegian police
closed in 1971 — and science reopened.

## Format & specs

- Full film: **~15–16 min**, 1280×720, 24 fps, H.264 + AAC, −16 LUFS
- 4 vertical shorts (1080×1920, ~55 s each): A. The Strange Suitcases · B. The Notebook
  and the Travel Trail · C. Was She a Spy? · D. The Identity That Remains Unknown
- Narration: measured, restrained, ~145 wpm; no invented dialogue; quotes are sourced
  verbatim from S2 or clearly marked paraphrases.

## Structure choice (of three candidates)

1. **"One file, one section"** — the police case number 134/70 as the spine; each
   chapter is a file tab pulled from the desk. *(CHOSEN — matches evidence-first tone;
   the file-tab motif gives natural chapter grammar and a strong cold-open device.)*
2. "The last nine days" — purely chronological reconstruction of her final days.
   Rejected: forces speculative connective tissue between sightings.
3. "The suspects" — theory-led. Rejected: front-loads speculation the record cannot
   support; violates claim-ledger discipline.

## Visual language — "the case file desk"

- Palette: ink charcoal background `#121417`; bone paper cards `#e9e2d3`; case amber
  `#c9a35c`; flag red `#a33c2f` (for DISPUTED/OPEN labels); cold slate `#5d7285`.
- Type: DejaVu Serif (titles), Carlito (body), DejaVu Sans Mono (file stamps, dates).
- Grammar: paper cards slide in on a dark desk; a case-tab marked **134/70** anchors
  every chapter; slow 1–2% pushes; drifting valley fog for Bergen exteriors; no gore,
  never depict the body — the death scene is shown as an annotated evidence diagram.
- Real images: Bergen/Isdalen/Ulriken/Møllendal photographs with on-screen attribution
  and an "ARCHIVE" or source tag; any reconstructed/illustrative visual is labelled
  **RECONSTRUCTION**. AI imagery never poses as an archival photo.
- Code-drawn recurring diagrams: Bergen map (Ulriken, Fløyen, Isdalen, station,
  hotels), Europe travel arcs, decoded-note strip, isotope/DNA result cards, timeline
  1970→2026.

## Sound

- Score: D minor, ~60 BPM pulse; low string-and-sine drone; sparse felt-piano plucks;
