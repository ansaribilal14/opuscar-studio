## Policy

- No image in this film is AI-generated. Vector illustrations are code-drawn and are
  always visually distinct (no faux-photo styling); labelled RECONSTRUCTION where they
  recreate evidence.
- The 1970 police sketch is embedded as a documentary artifact with its original
  caption visible; it is never presented as a photograph of the woman.
- Landscape and street photos are modern file photos used for atmosphere/geography,
  always carrying an on-screen "FILE PHOTO" credit so nothing reads as 1970 imagery.
- Møllendal cemetery scenes remain vector-only (no substitute cemetery photo is used,
  to avoid misrepresenting the location).
- Rejected on policy: all Alamy results (watermarks), all StockCake results
  (AI-generated), knit_texture.jpg (unusable exposure).
