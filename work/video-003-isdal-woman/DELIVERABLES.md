## YouTube description (verbatim)

In November 1970, a burned body was found in Norway's Isdalen valley, outside Bergen.
Her clothes had no labels. Her bottles had no marks. She had travelled Norway under at
least eight false names. The police closed the case in 1971 — probable suicide — but
many of their own investigators never believed it. More than fifty years later, her
name is still unknown.

This film follows the evidence: the scene, the suitcases, the coded notebook, the
hotel trail, the autopsy, the theories — and the isotope and DNA science that reopened
the file in 2016. Theories are labelled as theories. Nothing is invented.

⚠️ THE EVIDENCE DOES NOT SETTLE EVERYTHING
The 1970 suicide verdict is contested. Espionage and murder remain unproven theories.
Two accounts used in this film (a 1991 taxi driver; a 2005 hiker) are unverified and
labelled as such on screen.
