# TREATMENT.md — video-002 "The Zodiac Killer: The Ciphers, the Letters, and the Killer Nobody Could Identify"

## Logline
A killer terrorized the San Francisco Bay Area in 1968–69, then did the unthinkable: he wrote to the newspapers, sent ciphers, and dared police to identify him — two of his ciphers took fifty-one years to break, and he has still never been named.

## Central question (per production order)
How could a killer leave investigators so much material to examine, have major ciphers decoded decades later, and still remain unidentified?
