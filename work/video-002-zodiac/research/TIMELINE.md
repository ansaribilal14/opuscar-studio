# TIMELINE.md — video-002 Zodiac (verified facts only)

## Pre-1968 (disputed connection)
- 1966-10-30 — Cheri Jo Bates murdered, Riverside. Connection to Zodiac DISPUTED (Riverside PD historically unconfirmed; Case Breakers 2021 claimed it, investigators rejected). [D1]

## The confirmed attacks (5 murdered, 2 survived)
- 1968-12-20 — Lake Herman Road, Benicia: David Faraday (17) and Betty Lou Jensen (17) shot and killed. [E2]
- 1969-07-04 — Blue Rock Springs Park, Vallejo: Darlene Ferrin (22) killed; Michael Mageau (19) survived, wounded. [E2]
- 1969-09-27 — Lake Berryessa, Napa County: Cecelia Shepard (22) died 1969-09-29; Bryan Hartnell (20) survived. Attacker wore an executioner-style hood with the crosshair symbol. [E2]
- 1969-10-11 — Presidio Heights, San Francisco: cab driver Paul Stine (29) killed; witnesses; partial print recovered from cab door. [E2]

## The correspondence
- 1969-07-31 — Three near-identical letters to SF Chronicle, SF Examiner, Vallejo Times-Herald, each with one part of a three-part cipher (408 symbols total); demanded publication. [E3]
- 1969-08-04 — "Debut of Zodiac" letter; the name "Zodiac" debuts. [E5]
- 1969-08-04/05 — 408 solution published (Hardens, Salinas); FBI independently corroborated. [E4]
- 1969-11-08 — 340-character cipher mailed to SF Chronicle. [E6]
- 1969-11-09 — "Bus bomb" letter (timeline anchor; not dramatized beyond mention)
- 1970-04-20 — Letter with 13-symbol cipher ("My name is —") + bomb diagram: Z13 UNSOLVED. [E7]
- 1970-06-26 — "Little List" letter with 32-symbol cipher + map/Mount Diablo button: Z32 UNSOLVED. [E7]

## Cryptanalysis milestones
- 1969-08 — 408 read: killer claims killings are "fun", promises more, refuses to give his name; final 18 characters "EBEORIETEMETHHPITI" unexplained. [E4]
- 2020-12-05 — 340 solution found by David Oranchak, Sam Blake, Jarl Van Eycke; submitted to FBI; FBI confirmed. Plaintext opens "I HOPE YOU ARE HAVING LOTS OF FUN IN TRYING TO CATCH ME…"; includes "IM NOT AFRAID OF THE GAS CHAMBER BECAUSE IT WILL SEND ME TO PARADICE ALL THE SOONER". [E6]
- 2020s — Z13/Z32 remain unsolved (underdetermined; many plausible readings). [E7]

## Investigation
- 1969-10 — SFPD sketch; partial print from Stine's cab door; no database match then or since (as documented). [E2/E9]
- 1991/1992 — Arthur Leigh Allen search warrant; Zodiac-brand watch among items; died 1992-08; never charged. Fingerprints, handwriting, later DNA did not match. [E9]
- 2002 — Partial DNA profile from saliva on letter stamps/envelopes; insufficient for definitive matching. [E11]
- 2021-10 — Case Breakers name Gary Francis Poste; FBI denies case solved; investigators reject claim; SFPD/SDPD restate case open. [E14/E15/E16]
- 2026 — Case remains officially open and unsolved. [E16]
