# FACT_CHECK — claim ledger + script wording rules

Status: **established** (multiple independent sources / primary source) ·
**secondary** (single reliable secondary source) · **proposed** (hypothesis in
literature) · **disputed** · **open**.
Only `established` claims may be stated as plain fact; `secondary` claims may be
stated with attribution-free but cautious wording; `proposed` must be labelled
("researchers have proposed…"); `open` items must be presented as unresolved.

## Disagreements / nuances to respect
- **Kraus 1420.3556 vs Ehman 1420.4556 MHz** (C09): not a scientific disagreement —
  an equipment-serial-number/typo accounting issue explained in S2. Film uses
  Ehman's value and may tell the typo story as color ("even the telescope's own
  local oscillator had been mislabelled").
- **37 s vs 72 s** (C05): the film explicitly debunks 37 s.
- **"Exactly the hydrogen line"** (C09): the film says "close to — but a hair
  above — the hydrogen line", per Ehman's number.

## Open questions the film must preserve
- What produced the signal remains **unresolved** (no confirmed terrestrial or
  astrophysical explanation; no replication).
- Whether it was a transient natural phenomenon or a transmission is **open**.
