# TIMELINE.md — video-004 dated timeline (all entries sourced per CLAIMS.md)

## 1959 — the expedition
- **1959-01-23** — Group leaves Sverdlovsk by train (Dyatlov's diary, A1).
- **1959-01-25/26** — Arrive Ivdel; onward to Vizhay (last settlement) by truck (A1).
- **1959-01-27/28** — Sled/hike up the Lozva into the Auspiya valley. **28 Jan: Yuri
  Yudin turns back** (illness) — his farewell is documented in diaries/testimony (A1).
- **1959-01-31** — Supply cache ("labaz") dug into the Auspiya valley bank; group
  continues north toward Otorten (A1).
- **1959-02-01 (day)** — Route deviates ~1 km west of the planned pass line; after
  midday the group begins climbing the open slope of Kholat Syakhl; decision (recon-
  structed from tracks/protocol) to camp on the slope at ~1,050 m. Last diary entry;
  last photographs show the tent going up (A1, camera films).
- **1959-02-01/02 (night)** — By reconstruction: tent cut open from inside; all nine
  descend ~1.5 km to the forest edge in severe frost and wind. Campsite slope ~23° ± 2°
  (P1). Official reconstruction ties the night to 1–2 Feb.

## The search
- **1959-02-20/21** — Group declared overdue; UPI-organized search parties depart (A1).
- **1959-02-26** — Slobtsov's group finds the tent: standing, supplies inside, canvas
  cut open from within; footprints lead downslope (A1 discovery protocol 28.II.59).
- **1959-02-26/27** — Cedar at the forest edge: broken branches, remains of a small
  fire, two bodies beneath (Doroshenko, Krivonishenko) (A1).
- **1959-02-28** — Dyatlov found on the line back toward the tent (A1).
- **1959-03-05** — Kolmogorova and Slobodin found closer to the tent (A1).
- **1959-05-04/05** — Ravine four found under ~4 m of snow in the creek ravine ~75 m
  from the cedar, near a dug-out shelter (A3).

## Forensics & case
- **1959-03-04/09** — Autopsies, first five: hypothermia; Slobodin's skull fracture
  judged non-fatal; hypothermia behaviour findings; burns on cedar victims (A2).
- **1959-05-09/11** — Autopsies, ravine four: fatal compression injuries (skull,
  chest) without external wounds; tongue absence documented (Dubinina) (A2).
- **1959-04-07/08** — Atmanaki testimony: glowing orange objects seen in the region's
  night sky (as testimony; A4).
- **1959-05-28** — Prosecutor Lev Ivanov closes the case: "a compelling natural force
  which they were unable to overcome" (A6).

## The long tail
- **1963** — The 1079 pass formally renamed "Dyatlov Pass" (widely documented).
- **2019–2020** — Prosecutors' re-examination: avalanche as trigger, hypothermia as
  cause, "force majeure", no crime (R1).
- **2021-01-28** — Gaume & Puzrin publish the delayed-slab mechanism (P1).
- **2022-03-24** — Follow-up expeditions: avalanches "not exceptional" at the pass (P2).

## Reconstructed night sequence (labelled as reconstruction when narrated)
1. Wind loads the slope above the tent through the evening (P1 mechanism).
2. ~9.5–13 h after the cut, the slab releases; occupants escape by cutting out (P1).
3. Descent to the forest line; fire under the cedar; two remain (documented outcome).
4. Attempts back toward the tent; three die on the line (documented positions).
5. Four shelter in the ravine den; die of combined injuries/cold (documented).
