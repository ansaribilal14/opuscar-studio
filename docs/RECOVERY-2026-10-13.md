## Lessons (now encoded in BRAIN.md §0/§5)

1. **Push after every step** — the biggest losses (videos, full film sources) existed
   only as local files or in a single remote location.
2. **One account must never be a single point of failure** — keep the brain in the repo,
   not in the account.
3. **Release assets are data** — for irreplaceable renders, also commit posters/SRT and
   frame sources; keep final MP4s in Releases but treat the repo as the source of truth
   so the MP4 can always be rebuilt.
